Business Acquisition FAQ

How do I buy a business?

Most acquisitions follow a process that includes identifying target businesses, reviewing financial information, conducting due diligence, arranging financing, negotiating terms, and completing the closing.

What is SDE?

Seller's Discretionary Earnings (SDE) is a common valuation metric used for small businesses. It represents the earnings available to a single owner-operator after normalizing owner expenses and compensation.

What is EBITDA?

EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization and is commonly used for larger businesses.

Can I use SBA financing to buy a business?

Qualified buyers may use SBA-backed financing for eligible acquisitions, subject to lender underwriting, borrower qualifications, collateral, and cash flow requirements.
