About

Shaheen Arif is the founder of getservices.ai and the editor of BusinessBuy, publishing buying guides about buying and selling small businesses. Shaheen has more than 10 years of experience in IT and project management.

How our guides are made

  • Picks are based on published retailer product information, practical buying criteria and use cases.
  • We do not invent ratings, test results, prices or claims, and we tell readers what to verify on the product page.
  • Guides link to independent sources where safety, rules or health are involved.
  • Some links are affiliate links: we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend.

What Shaheen covers on BusinessBuy

Guides on BusinessBuy focus on buying and selling small businesses, written for US small business buyers and sellers. Each guide sets out who an option suits, what to compare between models, and what to confirm on the retailer's page, such as current price, sizing, delivery and returns, before you order.
