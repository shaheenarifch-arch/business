Frequently Asked Questions

Common business acquisition questions.

Concise educational answers for buyers beginning a search or evaluating a specific opportunity.

How do I buy a business?

Start by defining your budget, geography, preferred industries, owner role, and financing constraints. Then source opportunities, review normalized cash flow, value the business, negotiate an LOI, complete due diligence, finalize financing and agreements, and plan the transition.

Can SBA financing be used to buy a business?

Qualified buyers may use SBA-backed financing for eligible acquisitions, subject to lender underwriting, borrower equity, cash flow, collateral, management experience, and current program requirements.

What should acquisition due diligence cover?

A diligence plan commonly covers tax returns, financial statements, bank records, contracts, leases, employees, customers, vendors, licenses, litigation, equipment, working capital, and seller representations.

How is a small business valued?

Small businesses are commonly evaluated using normalized SDE or EBITDA, market multiples, assets, growth, customer concentration, owner dependence, capital requirements, and business-specific risk.
