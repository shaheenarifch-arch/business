Frequently Asked Questions

Business acquisition resource FAQ.

Concise answers to common first-time buyer questions. Professional advice may be required for a specific transaction.

What resources does BusinessBuy.site provide?

BusinessBuy.site publishes educational guides covering business valuation, due diligence, SBA and seller financing, deal sourcing, broker selection, acquisition timelines, and regional markets.

How is a small business valued?

Small businesses are commonly evaluated using normalized SDE or EBITDA, market multiples, assets, growth, customer concentration, owner dependence, capital requirements, and business-specific risks.

Can SBA financing be used to acquire a business?

Qualified buyers may use SBA-backed financing for eligible acquisitions, subject to lender underwriting, borrower equity, cash flow, collateral, management experience, and current program requirements.

What should acquisition due diligence cover?

A diligence plan commonly covers tax returns, financial statements, bank records, contracts, leases, employees, customers, vendors, licenses, litigation, equipment, working capital, and seller representations.
