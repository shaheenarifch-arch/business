About BusinessBuy.site

Helping buyers make better-informed acquisition decisions.

BusinessBuy.site provides educational resources for entrepreneurs researching, evaluating, financing, and acquiring established businesses.

Our purpose

BusinessBuy.site helps prospective buyers understand the major stages of acquiring a business, from defining investment criteria and screening opportunities to valuation, financing, due diligence, negotiation, closing, and transition planning.

What we publish

  • Business valuation guidance covering Seller's Discretionary Earnings, EBITDA, normalized add-backs, cash flow quality, and market multiples.
