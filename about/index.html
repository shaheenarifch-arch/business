About us

Plain-English guides for people buying an established business

BusinessBuy.site helps first-time and experienced buyers research, value, finance and close on a small business, without the jargon.

In short

BusinessBuy.site is an independent educational site about buying an existing small business. We explain valuation (SDE and EBITDA), financing (SBA loans and seller financing), due diligence and country-specific buying rules for the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and we link to live listings on World Businesses for Sale. We are a GetServices.ai company, founded and edited by Shaheen Arif.

Why we exist

Buying a business is a big decision. Good information should be easy to find.

Buying a business that already has customers, staff and cash flow can be a faster and less risky route to ownership than starting from scratch. But the process is full of unfamiliar terms, financing rules and checks that most people only go through once or twice in their lives. Much of the information online is written by brokers, lenders or sellers, each with their own interest in the deal.

We started BusinessBuy.site to give buyers one place to understand how acquisitions actually work: what a fair price looks like, how deals are financed, what to verify before you sign, and how the rules change from one country to another. Every guide is written to help you ask better questions of the professionals you hire, not to replace them.

How we work

Our editorial standards

Our guides are educational. We do not give legal, tax, lending or investment advice, and we always recommend working with a qualified accountant, attorney and lender on your deal.

  • Sourced, not invented. We base guides on public sources such as SBA program information, industry associations and published marketplace data, and we do not make up figures, ratings or case studies.
  • Ranges, not promises. Valuation multiples, margins and timelines vary by industry, location and deal. We give typical ranges and explain what moves a number up or down.
  • Kept current. Financing rules and market conditions change. We review guides and update them when we find something out of date.
  • Corrections welcome. If you spot an error, email agents@getservices.ai and we will review it.
