Quick answer

Buying a business follows six stages: find a business that fits your criteria, value it, arrange financing, set up the company that will own it, complete due diligence, and close the deal. BusinessBuy.site has a free resource for each stage, plus live listings from World Businesses for Sale. We do not charge buyers any fees.

Who these are for

Free help for every buyer

These resources are written for first-time buyers moving from a job into ownership, experienced owners adding another business, and international buyers looking at the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Each one explains the stage in plain English, so you can ask better questions of the brokers, lenders, accountants and attorneys you work with.
