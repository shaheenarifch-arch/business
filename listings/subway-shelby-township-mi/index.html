About this business

This Subway has been running since 1988 and is fully renovated. It sits in a busy strip center surrounded by schools, a new stadium, churches and growing residential neighborhoods, which keeps traffic coming through lunch, after school and on game days.

It can be run semi-absentee. The current owner works about 25–30 hours a week, with 1 full-time and 3 part-time employees. The owner is selling for family and personal reasons, not because of problems with the store.

A quick look at the numbers

At $99,000 against $45,000 of owner benefit, the price is about 2.2× SDE. That is in the usual range for small food franchise resales. Before you make an offer, plan for the $3,500 transfer fee, working capital, and any lender requirements. For a full breakdown of sales, rent, royalties and payback, read Subway at $99K: the numbers explained.

How to buy this franchise

  1. Contact the seller by email and say who you are, how you plan to pay and your timeline.
  2. Sign the NDA. You then get the store name, address and financial records.
  3. Review the numbers and visit the store as a customer. Use our due diligence checklist.
  4. Arrange financing if needed. See SBA and seller financing options.
  5. Get franchisor approval. Subway must approve the new owner and the transfer. Our Subway resale guide for Metro Detroit explains the steps.
  6. Close and train: complete the franchise training and 2 weeks of on-site training with the seller.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Shelby Township Subway listed for?

The asking price is $99,000. The franchise transfer fee of $3,500, which includes training, is extra.

How much does the store make?

The seller reports $295,000 in annual sales and $45,000 a year in owner benefit, with the owner working about 25–30 hours a week.

How long is the lease and the franchise agreement?

The lease runs to March 2028 with one 5-year option. The franchise agreement has 15 years left.

Not necessarily. Subway must approve the buyer, and the deal includes franchise training plus 2 weeks of on-site training from the seller.
