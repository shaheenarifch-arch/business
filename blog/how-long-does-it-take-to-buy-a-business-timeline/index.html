How Long Does It Take to Buy a Business? 6-9 Month Timeline from Search to Close

TL;DR — The Real Timeline to Buy a Business

Most small business acquisitions take 6-9 months from first search to funded close. Fastest SBA deals close in 90-120 days if lender pre-qualified and data room ready. Slow deals stretch to 12 months due to landlord, third-party reports, or seller cold feet. Breakdown: Month 1-2 Search & Pre-Qual, Month 2-3 IOI/LOI, Month 3-4 Due Diligence & Quality of Earnings, Month 4-6 SBA Underwriting & Valuation, Month 6-7 Close & Transition. Pre-qual before you search, have your M&A attorney on standby, and demand a full data room in LOI.

Everyone wants to know how long it takes to buy a business. Brokers say 3 months. TikTok says 30 days. Reality for SBA 7(a) buyers in 2026 is 6-9 months if you do it right — and rushing the wrong parts is how you lose your deposit or buy a bad deal.

This guide is the actual timeline we see for $500k to $5M Main Street and lower-middle-market deals funded by SBA 7(a), seller financing, or a combo. No theory. Just what lenders, attorneys, and Quality of Earnings firms require today.

1. Month 1-2: Search, Criteria & Lender Pre-Qual [Where Most People Waste 6 Months]

If you start searching before you have capital strategy, you are browsing, not buying. Sellers and business brokers prioritize buyers with a lender pre-qual letter.

Week 1-2: Define Your Buy Box

  • Budget: SBA buyers need 10% down minimum. $1M purchase = $100k cash + $15k-30k closing buffer. Full SBA stack with seller note as equity injection can go to 0-5% down only if seller note is on full standby for >5 years.
  • SDE Model: Target Seller's Discretionary Earnings $200k-$750k for your first deal. Avoid sub-$120k SDE unless owner-operator with low complexity.
  • Geography & Skills: 90-minute drive max for first acquisition unless you have general manager in place.
  • No-Go List: Restaurants with <4 years lease, customer concentration >35% single client, owner is sales, undocumented cash >15%.

Week 2-4: Get SBA Pre-Qualified Before IOIs

This is the #1 accelerator. Lenders today want 3 things in 48 hours:

  • Personal Financial Statement (SBA Form 413), 2 years tax returns, resume
  • Proof of liquidity: bank statements + retirement account summary for 10% + reserves
  • Buy-box summary: industries you will buy, deal size, transition plan

Ask your lender: "Can you issue a pre-qual letter with max total project size and pre-approved equity requirement?" Good SBA banks issue this in 3-5 days. Without it, brokers will not take you seriously.

Week 3-8: Build a 50-Deal NDA Pipeline

Use your sponsored marketplace links to triage. Contact 10-15 brokers/week.

Track each deal in a simple CRM: Listing ID, SDE, multiple, lease years, landlord transfer clause, reason for sale, broker responsiveness. Kill 90% before IOI.

2. Month 2-3: IOI to LOI — Lock Exclusivity the Right Way

Indication of Interest (IOI) is non-binding price range. Letter of Intent (LOI) is the 3-5 page document that gets you exclusivity, starts diligence clock, and triggers third-party costs.

DocumentPurposeBinding?What to Include
IOIGet management call, test seller expectationsNon-binding priceRange $900k-$1.1M, 80% SBA 10yr, 10% seller note standby, 10% equity
LOILock deal, secure exclusivityMostly non-binding except confidentiality, exclusivity 60-90 days, no-shopPrice, structure, contingencies, due diligence checklist access, working capital target, non-compete

LOI Checklist (Must Have in 2026)

  • Price + Structure: Enterprise value $X, includes inventory $Y excluded/included, W/C peg (e.g., $80k). Breakdown: SBA loan $Z, Seller note $A on full standby 10 years, Buyer equity $B.
  • Contingencies: Financing, lease assignment with at least 4 years remaining, QOE verification within 15% of reported SDE, license transferability.
  • Due Diligence Access: Data room within 3 business days: 3-year tax returns (business + personal if S-Corp/LLC), QuickBooks GL, payroll reports, merchant statements, top 20 customers/vendors by revenue last 24 months, lease + amendments, debt schedule, asset list, employee census (wage, role, tenure).
  • Exclusivity & Timeline: 75-day exclusivity, close 45 days after SBA commitment letter, seller will not solicit others.
  • Earn-Out Language: If used, capped at 15-20% of deal and tied to gross revenue, not EBITDA, with clear calculation and quarterly reporting.
LOI Negotiation Script for Seller Cold Feet: "Sarah, we love the business and we're comfortable at $1.2M if QOE holds. Two asks to move fast: 1) 75-day exclusivity so I can spend $18k on QOE/valuation without risk. 2) You stay 30 days post-close + 10 hrs/week for 60 days after for relationships. In return, I'll waive equipment re-inspection if QOE passes and increase seller note interest from 6% to 7% on standby." Why this works: You trade a low-cost concession (interest on money you aren't paying yet) for high-value certainty (data room, exclusivity).

3. Month 3-4: Due Diligence & Quality of Earnings — Where Bad Deals Die

This phase decides if you proceed or kill. Budget $12k-$35k for outside diligence on sub-$3M deals. Do not skip QOE to save $8k.

Data Room Ready List (Send to Seller Day 1 of LOI)

  • Financials: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 YTD P&L + BS + Tax Returns (1120S/1065 + K1s), debt schedule, AR/AP aging
  • HR: Payroll register 24 months, W2/W3, benefits summary, contractor agreements, handbook, open comp claims
  • Sales: CRM export of jobs/clients last 24 months, top 20 by revenue + margin, sales contracts, warranties
  • Legal: Articles, EIN letter, cap table, all leases, all loans, litigation letters, insurance loss runs
  • Operations: Asset list with VIN/serial + lien holders, inventory count method, SOPs, licenses, certifications

If seller cannot produce this in 5 days, pause QOE clock. Delays here compress bank time later.

4. Month 4-6: SBA Underwriting, Business Valuation & Third-Party Reports

This is where 70% of delays happen. Bank cannot submit to SBA without valuation from an SBA-qualified appraiser, and landlord consent.

What Lender Needs After QOE

  • Business Valuation: SBA requires independent valuation if total loan > $500k. Cost $3.5k-$7k, takes 10-15 business days after they receive QOE + tax returns. Order on Day 1 of diligence.
  • Environmental: If real estate included or gas station/dry cleaner/autos, Phase I $2k-$4k.
  • Lease Assignment: Draft assignment + landlord estoppel + non-disturbance language. Landlords take 2-4 weeks. This is #1 hidden killer.
  • Entity + Insurance: Newco LLC/Corp, EIN, operating account, insurance binder showing lender as loss payee, life insurance assignment if required.
  • Background & Credit: All guarantors >20% ownership need personal credit >680 ideally, no recent BK.

Negotiation Script for Landlord

Landlord Call Script: "Hi Jim — I'm acquiring [Business] at [Center]. Lease has 3.5 years left. I intend to honor all terms, same use, same staff. Request: 1) Assignment to Newco with my personal guaranty replacing seller's 2) Option to extend 5 years at fair market (we'll invest $40k in improvements) 3) Assignment fee capped at $1,500. My lender [Bank] needs estoppel by [Date]. I can pay Jan rent early to show goodwill. Can your attorney send assignment draft by Friday?" Attach: Buyer bio, personal financial strength letter (not full PFS), business plan 1-pager.

5. Month 6-7: Final Docs, Funding & Transition Day

Once bank issues commitment letter, you have typically 30-45 days to close. This is document heavy but predictable if you prepare.

Closing Checklist

  • Purchase Agreement (APA): Asset vs stock, representations & warranties survival 12-18 months, indemnity cap at 10-20% seller note, non-compete 3 yrs / 25 miles, training hours defined (e.g., 80 hrs over 30 days).
  • Funding Flow: SBA loan wires to escrow, seller note executed with full standby agreement (SBA Form 155), buyer equity wired, UCC liens released.
  • Licenses & Bank Accounts: Merchant account, DBA filing, sales tax permit, vendor accounts assignment.
  • Transition Plan: Employee letter: "New owner, same team, same pay." Customer letter within 48 hrs. Meet top 10 customers in first 2 weeks.

Speed-Up Protocol: How to Close in 4 Months (If You Must)

  • Get pre-qual + attorney retainer paid before first NDA. Have engagement letters signed: M&A attorney, QOE CPA, SBA bank.
  • Disqualify fast: If recast SDE can't be verified by tax returns in 7 days, kill.
  • Run diligence and valuation in parallel, not sequentially. Most buyers waste 3 weeks waiting.
  • Landlord intro on Day 2 of LOI, not Week 4.
  • Data room rule: If >40% of checklist missing at Day 7, you pause exclusivity clock.

Buying a business is not hard because of finance. It's hard because of coordination. Your job is to keep five parties moving: seller, broker, lender, landlord, and appraiser. Miss one weekly update and you add two weeks.

If you are starting now, use this order: (1) Lender pre-qual letter, (2) NDA pipeline from verified marketplace, (3) LOI with data room + landlord language, (4) Parallel QOE + valuation, (5) Close with transition letter templates ready.
