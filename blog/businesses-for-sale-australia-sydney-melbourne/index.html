2) Foreign Buyer Playbook: 188 Business Innovation Visa & FIRB in 2026

The former 188A/B/C program closed July 2024 and transitioned to the National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858) Business Innovation stream. State nomination remains central. If you are non-resident, you need both a visa strategy and Foreign Investment Review Board (FIRB) approval for business acquisitions.

FIRB basics for business purchase

  • General threshold: $330M enterprise value for non-sensitive; $0 for sensitive, agribusiness $15M+ cumulative.
  • Any purchase where foreign person acquires substantial interest (20%+) or effective control = notifiable.
  • Fee: $4,200-$55,100 depending on deal size (indexed). Decision 30-90 days. Conditional approval common.
  • Exemptions: Australian citizens, PRs, New Zealand citizens under special category 444 exempt.

188 legacy holders still settling

If you held 188A, you must show $200k transfer and ongoing management. When buying, structure purchase agreement as "transfer of business as a going concern" to qualify for GST-free treatment under ATO GSTR 2002/5 – essential for visa cashflow proof.

Checklist — FIRB + Visa Documents: Passport, CV, 2 years business financials, net asset proof (bank + property), draft business plan (Sydney location, hiring plan), source of funds, police clearance, lease draft, sale contract subject-to clauses.

How to get bankable in 14 days

  • Get 3-year P&L, FY24/25 tax returns, BAS QTR, debtors/creditors ledger, asset list, lease, employee contracts.
  • Structure: Entity = Pty Ltd as trustee for unit trust. ABN/ACN ready.
  • Security: Residential property LVR <80% gives 70% lend. No property = 40-50% max + seller finance.
  • Seller finance script: "We are bank-approved for 50%. Would you consider 20% vendor finance at 6% over 24 months, paid from profits, secured by second-ranking PPSR? That gets you full price and faster settlement."

State Stamp Duty Notes

  • NSW: No duty on business assets except land/lease premium. Transfer of lease may attract premium duty if premium paid – check Revenue NSW OSR.
  • VIC: Business sale duty abolished 2009, but sale of land/business with land interest triggers duty. Leases >1yr may attract 0.5% lease duty historically removed – now generally nil unless marketable security.
  • QLD: No duty on business assets post 2009 except transfer of business land or QLD business assets that are dutiable property. Transfer duty on retail lease assignment = nil unless premium.
  • WA: $0 duty for goodwill, but business with license/land – duty applies. Always get duty advice if freehold + business bundled (car wash freehold!).
  • Going concern GST: If seller is registered, buyer is registered, and sale contract identifies going concern + all necessary assets + ongoing enterprise – GST-free. Add clause 9 in standard contract.

Lease assignment – make or break in NSW/VIC

  • Retail lease assignment needs landlord consent under Retail Leases Act – landlord cannot unreasonably withhold, 28-42 days to respond.
  • Must provide disclosure statement, financials, bank reference, business plan. Shopping centres (Westfield, Scentre, Mirvac) require 2-3 month approval.
  • Personal guarantee: Limit to 2 years or capped amount. Negotiate bank guarantee = 3-6 months rent, not 12.
  • Transfer of business date = settlement + assignment registration. Add clause: "Settlement conditional on unconditional lease assignment and landlord estoppel for no breach/outgoings."

5) Due Diligence & Transfer: 48-Point Checklist to Avoid Buying a Job

Financial

  • 3 years tax returns + ATO Integrated Client Account, BAS vs P&L variance <5%
  • MYOB/Xero direct read-only access – check non-recurring add-backs
  • Debtors >90 days, staff entitlements ($ leave loading), WorkCover claims
  • Equipment: PPSR search, REVS check, warranty transfer

Legal & Compliance

  • ABN verification, business name transfer, council approvals (food license NSW Food Authority, QLD liquor etc.)
  • Franchise disclosure if franchise – 14-day cooling off
  • Employee: Fair Work transfer of business – instrument assignment, consultation
  • Transfer of business as going concern checklist for GST-free

Pro tip: Use solicitors experienced in Sale of Business contracts – not conveyancers. In NSW, Law Society standard Business Sale contract; in VIC, LIV/REIV Business Sale Agreement. Always request landlord estoppel, staff entitlement audit, and ATO SGC super clearance – 68% of failed sales in our review collapsed on unpaid super or award underpayment, not price. CBA and NAB will now require Single Touch Payroll audit and WorkCover certificate before drawdown, so get these on day 1.

Financing edge for migrants: If you lack Australian property, pair ANZ GoBiz 40% + 30% vendor finance + 30% cash. ANZ loves to see 6 months mortgage or rent ledger and 12-month business bank statements from overseas if translated via NAATI. Offer director guarantee capped at loan amount, not unlimited, and add clause to release guarantee after 24 months of on-time repayment – lenders agree if DSCR remains above 1.75x.
