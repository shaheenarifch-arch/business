Business Acquisition Guides & Insights

BusinessBuy publishes free, independent guides for people buying or selling a small business. Each guide answers one practical question: how to value a business, how to finance the deal, what to check in due diligence, and which mistakes cost buyers money.

Our guides draw on public sources such as the SBA, marketplace data and real listings. We do not invent figures, and we tell you what to verify with your accountant, attorney or lender. Before you make an offer

  • Ask for at least three years of tax returns and P&L statements, and recast them to find true owner earnings (SDE). How to value a business
  • Get financing pre-qualified early; SBA 7(a) loans usually need about 10% down. Financing guide
  • Never close without full due diligence on financials, the lease, licences and legal liabilities. 28-point due diligence checklist
