2. Legal & Compliance Due Diligence — 6 Points

One lawsuit or missing IP assignment can void your asset purchase. Budget $1,500-$3,000 for lawyer review.

  • 11. Active/Pending Lawsuits & Threats — Request: 5-year litigation log + insurance claims. Search PACER, county court, BBB complaints. Pass: Zero open wage claims, class actions, OSHA recordables >3. Fail: Any open >$25k or pattern of wage theft/sexual harassment — walk, SBA won't fund.
  • 12. UCC Liens, Tax Liens, Judgments — Run UCC search in incorporation state + business location state. IRS transcript via Form 4506-C. Secretary of State good standing cert. Must be clear at close or paid from escrow.
  • 13. Entity Structure, Cap Table & Contracts Assignability — Articles, EIN, operating agreement, cap table, profit distribution history. Review all contracts >$10k/yr for "change of control" or "consent required" clauses. 70% of government contracts are NOT assignable without re-bid — huge trap.
  • 14. Intellectual Property Ownership — Trademark USPTO search, domain WHOIS, logo/designer assignment letter, software code assignment (did owner actually assign his contractor's work?). Ask: "Was any software/brand built by freelancer? Show IP assignment." No assignment = you don't own it.
  • 15. Licenses, Certifications & Compliance — HVAC license, plumbing journeyman, food permit, alcohol license (Type 47/48 in California non-transferable), childcare license. Check: Is license in owner's name personally? Then you must re-qualify. Add 90 days to timeline.
  • 16. Data Privacy, Employee Compliance — If business stores customer PII, CCPA/GDPR policy, breach history. For staff: I-9 audit, worker comp MOD rate <1.0, independent contractor misclassification (1099 vs W-2) — California, NJ, MA aggressively fine. IRS SS-8 issue.

Checklist Script for Attorney Call

Send your M&A attorney: “Please confirm: (a) asset purchase clears all liens via UCC-3, (b) IP assignment schedule Exhibit A, (c) non-compete 3yr/25 miles enforceable in this state, (d) change-of-control consents listed in disclosure schedule. Flat fee for APA review?”

4. Commercial & Customer Due Diligence — 6 Points

Revenue quality > revenue size. One-time project revenue at 4x is worthless vs 1x recurring contracts.

  • 23. Customer Concentration & Top-10 List — Get last 24mo customer revenue rank, % of revenue. Pass: No customer >15% (SBA prefers <20%), Top-10 <40%. Fail: >30% one customer = require 50% earn-out or walk. Call top 5 customers: "Have you considered leaving? Who is your contact post-sale?"
  • 24. Churn, Retention & Recurring Revenue % — Calculate logo churn and dollar churn. For subscription/home services: monthly churn <4% good, >7% bad. What % of revenue is contracted recurring (maintenance contracts, subscriptions) vs one-time? 60%+ recurring = +0.5x multiple justified. Request: SaaS cohort report, or cleaning routes retention sheet.
  • 25. Reviews, Brand & Lead Engine — Pull Google Reviews (count, recency, owner reply rate), Yelp, Glassdoor. Check Google Business Profile suspended history. Lead source breakdown: referral vs Google Ads vs LSA vs marketplace. If >50% leads come from one portal (Thumbtack, DoorDash, Amazon), you are renting an audience — risk up. Ask for ad accounts access read-only.
  • 26. Pricing Power & Competition Moat — Last 2 price increases dates + % + churn after. Can you raise 5% day one? Test: call competitor as secret shopper. Look for license moat (plumbing, electrical), contract moat (3yr commercial cleaning), or brand moat (4.8* 400 reviews). No moat + commodity = cap multiple at 2.5x SDE.
  • 27. Pipeline & Backlog Quality — Open proposals, win rate %, backlog months. Service: 2-3 months ideal. Too much backlog (6+ months) often means underpriced or understaffed. Too little (<2 weeks) means sales gap post-close.
  • 28. Geography & Channel Risk — Where does revenue come from physically? SBA collateral location. If home services route radius >45 min, labor inefficiencies + fuel + churn spike. Map customers in Google My Maps. Also platform risk: Amazon FBA suspension rate, Shopify chargeback rate >1% fail.

Customer Call Script (Use After LOI, Before Close)

"Hi, I'm Shahaan, buying [Business] from [Owner] who is retiring after 12 years. We're keeping all staff. Quick 3 min: what keeps you with us? If you could improve one thing, what? Would you be open to a 12-month renewal at same pricing?" Do 10 calls, log notes. If 3+ say they will leave without owner — renegotiate price -20% or earn-out.

5. The 2026 Due Diligence Playbook: Red Flags, Data Room & Timeline

A) Top 12 Red Flags — Walk Immediately

  • Add-backs >30% of SDE inflated with "my wife's Tesla is marketing"
  • Tax returns show $0 profit but asking 4x SDE = fraud
  • Owner works 65 hrs, no manager, no SOPs — you buy a job
  • Customer concentration >40% or 1 client = 60% revenue
  • Lease <24 months + no option + landlord won't talk early
  • Cash business with no POS and bank deposits don't match
  • Open IRS/state tax lien, MCA stacking (2+ advances), EIDL default
  • Key employee is brother/spouse who will leave post-sale
  • Equipment >12 years, no maintenance log, near replacement cliff $150k
  • Google Reviews <4.2 or 25+ reviews filtered in last 90 days deleted? Manipulation
  • Seller refuses QOE or bank statements — classic concealment
  • Supplier can cancel without cause on change of control — COGS spike risk

Your job is not to find a perfect business; it's to not buy a bad one.

Negotiation Holdback Language to Use:
"Based on DD finding [AR 90+ $23k uncollectible / equipment needs $18k repair / top customer -12% last quarter], we propose: price same $650k but $50k held in escrow 90 days post-close released upon AR collection / equipment repaired / customer retains >95%. Plus seller note 10% on standby for lender. If escrow claim, we keep. This protects both of us and gets us to close Friday."

This converts a price fight into a mechanism — sellers accept escrow 3x more than price cut.
