4. State-Specific Guides: California, Texas, Florida, New York

These four states = 48.3% of U.S. small business transactions in 2025-2026. Each has quirks.

California (19.2% of deals) — High SDE, High Regulation

  • Avg Multiple: 2.6x-3.8x SDE for services (0.4x higher than national due to scarcity)
  • Best Bets: HVAC in Inland Empire, IT MSP in Orange County, e-commerce + warehouse in LA, pool service statewide.
  • Gotchas: ABC test for contractors (AB5), Cal/OSHA higher workers comp, $800 LLC tax + 8.84% S-corp tax. Require 3 years CA tax returns Form 100S.
  • Negotiation script: “Given CA labor compliance overhead and AB5 reclassification risk on your 1099 cleaners, can we structure 85% at close, 15% earnout tied to employee retention past 6 months?”

Texas (12.1% of deals) — No Income Tax, Growth Beast

  • Avg Multiple: 2.4x-3.6x SDE, lower because more supply of trades.
  • Best Bets: HVAC, electrical, plumbing in DFW/Houston (huge new construction). Car wash in Austin suburbs. Medical cleaning with hospital contracts.
  • Gotchas: Property tax 1.8-2.2%, verify TDLR licenses transferrable, flood zone insurance if near Gulf.
  • Due diligence list: TDLR license #, truck titles no lien, customer contracts with assignability clause, workers comp mod <1.1.

Florida (10.4% of deals) — Absentee Paradise

  • Avg Multiple: 2.2x-3.3x SDE
  • Best Bets: Residential cleaning (Tampa, Orlando – snowbird second homes), lawn care, boat detailing/rentals in Fort Lauderdale, home watch services.
  • Gotchas: Hurricane insurance on trucks, seasonal dip June-Sept, non-compete enforceability limited (must be reasonable). 30% of sellers are retiring transplants – flexible financing common.
  • Filter tip: On BizBuySell, search “owner relocating north” = often below market.

New York (6.6% of deals) — High Barrier, High Moat

  • Avg Multiple: 2.8x-4.2x SDE (NYC premium)
  • Best Bets: Commercial cleaning in Manhattan/Brooklyn (24/7 office contracts), last-mile courier in Queens, childcare in Westchester (waitlists 12+ months).
  • Gotchas: NYC licensing (DCA), $15.50 minimum wage statewide $16 NYC, scaffold law insurance makes GC roofing insurance 3x national.
  • SBA note: Many NYC banks require seller standby 10% + personal residence within 100 miles.
