1. Laundromat - The Cash Cow King

Net: 30-40%Multiple: 3.0-4.0x SDEPrice: $150k-$600kSBA: Yes

Why #1 for first-time buyers? No inventory, no receivables, cash business, recession-proof. Average 25-year-old laundromat in Sun Belt: Revenue $280k, SDE $110k, sells at 3.3x = $363k. Owner works 10 hrs/week.

Due diligence: Water bills (should be 10-15% of revenue), machine age (check Dexter/Speed Queen age), lease (10+ yrs remaining), utility submeter trick. Re-cast SDE - add back owner phone, car, one-time repair.

💰
ROI Example: $363k purchase, 10% down $36k, SBA payment $5,200/mo. Cash flow after debt $4,000/mo = $48k/yr on $36k = 133% cash-on-cash.

Due Diligence Checklist (Steal This)

Use this for any business you buy from your folders:

  • 3 years P&L + Tax Returns (must match)
  • Recast SDE: add back owner salary, personal car, one-time legal, excess rent
  • Lease: 5+ years + options, assignable, no demolition clause
  • Customer concentration: no client >15%
  • Owner dependency: can it run 2 weeks without owner?
  • Equipment list + liens (UCC search)
  • SBA pre-qual: personal credit 680+, 10% liquid

FAQ

Can I buy a business with no money down?
Yes - SBA 7(a) requires 10% total, but seller can finance 10%, so you bring 5%. Some sellers finance 20-30% if business is strong. Search your folder how-to-buy-business-no-money-down-sba-seller-financing.
What multiple should I pay?
Main Street $0-$2M revenue: 2.0-4.0x SDE. Lower middle $2M-$10M: 3.0-6.0x EBITDA. Over 4.5x SDE only if recurring contracts, license moat, or real estate included.
How long does it take to buy a business?
Average 6-9 months: 1-2 months search, 1 month LOI + lender, 45 days due diligence + closing. SBA adds 30-45 days. See timeline article in your blog.
Are laundromats still profitable in 2026?
Yes - avg 30-40% net, cash, low labor, recession-proof. New competition is wash-dry-fold + pickup. Older mats with card systems and pickup are worth 0.5x more.
